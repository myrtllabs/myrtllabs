Ezekiel & Ezariah's
Animal & Reptile Safari! 🦁🐊

Team vs team — guess the animal by its sound or picture!

🎧 Sound rounds — listen and shout the animal!

🖼️ Picture rounds — see it and name it!

💃 Silly breaks — dance, freeze & funny faces!

🌴 Party screen
Best on a big screen • 6 teams • about 15–20 minutes • 👆 tap to turn on party sounds

Set Up the Teams 🐾

How many teams?
6
Turns per team
2
👀 How to play (for the grown-up)

1. Teams take turns. The team whose turn it is gets first guess.
2. Press Play Sound (or show the picture). Let the kids shout their guess!
3. Press Reveal to show the answer.
4. Tap the team that got it right to give +1 point (any team can steal!). Tap No one if nobody guessed.
5. Every few turns there's a silly break — dance, freeze, funny faces! 🎉
⌨️ Keyboard: Space = play / reveal / continue, R = replay sound, 1–6 = give a team the point, 0 = no one.
